The Eureka Method

We sell jobs booked, not “brand impressions.”

Most trades agencies hide behind reach metrics because they can’t tie spend to revenue. We start the other way around: pick the number that pays the bills, then work back.

  1. 1

    Lock the metric before the contract.

    Cost-per-booked-job is the only KPI on our dashboards. Everything else is diagnostic.

  2. 2

    Instrument before we optimize.

    Call tracking, form attribution, CRM sync — in place month one, or we don’t turn on ads.

  3. 3

    Quarterly cleanup, no contract lock-in.

    Month-to-month after the first 90 days. If the number isn’t moving, you walk — we keep the tracking we built.
